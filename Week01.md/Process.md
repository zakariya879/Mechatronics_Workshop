# Tools and Materials

1. Fr4 copper plate.

2. A metallic marker.
   <img width="1280" height="720" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-10-07 at 17 37 21" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ae25214e-59c1-4941-87d1-2adee98ef9a0" />

3. Ferric chloride acid.

4. Fine steel wool.

# Steps

### 1. Clean the copper plate
Sand the copper plate with the Fine steel wool until it becomes shiny and clean.

### 2. Cut the copper plate
Cut the copper plate with a utility knife cutter (or a rotary tool) to make three pieces with an area of at least 5 cm x 5 cm each.

### 3. Write
Write on the copper plate using the metallic marker.
<img width="1280" height="720" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-10-09 at 18 24 30" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/65d9c1e8-97ff-4049-af83-f0212af14c55" />

### 4. Dilute and remove the copper with acid
Dilute the ferric chloride acid at a 2:1 ratio (2 parts water to 1 part ferric chloride), put the copper pieces in the acid for 5-10 minutes, check every few minutes and pull the board when copper is gone.
<img width="864" height="1152" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-10-07 at 17 37 24" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4ce61c54-7369-404a-9142-b57c4a21cd3e" />

### 5. Pull out
Take them out, wash with water, and let them dry.
<img width="1152" height="864" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-10-07 at 17 37 22" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4071531b-15d4-49f0-9e13-be557a6934fa" />

### 6. Sand the metallic marker
Sand off the metallic marker with the fine steel wool.
<img width="1152" height="864" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-10-07 at 17 37 21" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e1909581-dc5e-46af-8a59-fd1281f13381" />

## Notes
1. Get a fine tip metallic marker to be able to draw with more detail.
2. Be careful when cutting the copper plate in order not to scratch the copper.
3. Cut from the copper side with the cutter.
4. Put the acid plastic trays/containers, not metal.
5. Wear nitrile gloves when dealing with the acid.
6. Don`t inhale the acid, nor the dust of the fiberglass Fr4.
7. Work in a ventilated area, away from kids/pets.
